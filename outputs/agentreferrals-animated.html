How it works

From intro to paid at closing.

Three steps, one signed agreement, and a fee you can see the whole way.

Step 1

Post the referral

Add the market, client type, price range and timeline. Set the fee you want, usually 20–35%.

Step 2

Choose your agent

Local agents apply with their production and reviews. You pick one and both sides sign the referral agreement.

Step 3

Get paid at closing

Status updates follow the client to contract and close. The fee is paid broker to broker through escrow.
